# 🌍 Atlas Sanctum — Witness Dashboard

## Proof of Regeneration

> **Not just intelligence. Proof of regeneration happening.**

The **Atlas Sanctum Witness Dashboard** is a grounded regenerative intelligence interface designed to show what is changing in reality — who is improving, where, why, and what evidence supports the change.

Instead of treating data as the product, the Witness Dashboard treats **observable change and evidence of outcomes** as the product.

```text
WHAT IS CHANGING IN REALITY RIGHT NOW?

Who is improving?
Where?
Why?
What changed?
What action caused the change?
What evidence proves it?
```

---

# 🧭 Core Philosophy

Every screen should answer one question:

> **“What is changing in reality right now?”**

Not simply:

> “What does the data say?”

But:

> **“Who is improving, where, and why?”**

The interface is therefore intentionally grounded in:

- Human visibility
- Cause → effect clarity
- Actionability
- Before → after evidence
- Field verification
- Farmer feedback
- Measurable regenerative outcomes

---


## 1. Ground Truth First

Evidence from the field should remain connected to every important claim.

## 2. Human Before Aggregate

A farmer should never disappear behind a dashboard metric.

## 3. Cause → Effect

Whenever possible, show:

```text
Condition
  →

  →

```

## 4. Proof Over Promise

Do not present a claim of regeneration without an evidence trail.

## 5. Actionable Intelligence

Every meaningful signal should have a clear next step where appropriate.

## 6. Progressive Complexity

Advanced intelligence should be available without making the core experience difficult to understand.

## 7. Low Cognitive Load

The platform should remain understandable under field and operational conditions.

# ⚠️ Data Integrity & Evidence Standards

The Witness Dashboard is explicitly designed around proof.

Production deployments should therefore distinguish between:

### Observed

Directly captured field evidence.

### Reported

Information provided by a farmer, field agent, or other source.

### Derived

A calculated value based on observations.

### Modeled

A prediction or estimate generated by a model.

### Verified

Evidence that has passed the defined verification workflow.

Example:

```text
OBSERVED
Photo + moisture reading

      ↓

REPORTED
Farmer feedback

      ↓

DERIVED
Soil score change

      ↓

MODELED
Projected yield signal

      ↓

VERIFIED
Approved evidence record
```

This distinction should be visible to users where it affects interpretation.

---

# 🔐 Governance

Because the system may contain farmer identities, locations, financial information, and field evidence, production versions should implement appropriate:

- Access control
- Data permissions
- Audit trails
- Evidence provenance
- Secure storage
- Consent mechanisms where applicable
- Role-based field access
- Data-retention rules

The platform should never make a consequential determination about an individual solely from an unverified model output.
